# Problem Statement

## Background

Many independent restaurants rely heavily on third-party delivery services to reach customers online. While these platforms make ordering convenient, they often become the primary point of interaction between customers and the restaurant.

The restaurant selected for this case study currently does not maintain a dedicated website. Customers primarily discover information through delivery applications, search engine listings, review platforms, and social media.

As a result, the restaurant has limited control over its digital presence and customer experience.

---

## The Problem

Customers looking for information about the restaurant must navigate multiple platforms to accomplish simple tasks such as viewing the menu, checking business hours, learning about the restaurant, or placing an order.

This fragmented experience creates unnecessary friction and makes it difficult for customers to develop a stronger connection with the restaurant's brand.

From a business perspective, the restaurant has limited opportunities to communicate its identity, highlight signature dishes, promote specials, or establish direct relationships with customers.

---

## User Problems

Based on initial observations, several potential user challenges were identified:

* Restaurant information is distributed across multiple platforms rather than being available in a single location.
* Menu browsing experiences vary depending on the platform being used.
* Customers may struggle to learn about the restaurant beyond basic ordering information.
* Important details such as hours, location, or featured dishes may not be immediately accessible.

These assumptions will be explored and validated throughout the research phase.

---

## Business Problems

The current digital presence presents several potential challenges for the restaurant:

* Limited control over branding and customer experience.
* Dependence on third-party platforms for online visibility.
* Fewer opportunities to communicate promotions or updates directly to customers.
* Difficulty building customer loyalty through owned digital channels.

---

## Project Goal

The goal of this project is to design a responsive website that provides customers with a centralized, user-friendly destination for discovering information, exploring the menu, and accessing ordering options.

The website should improve usability for customers while helping the restaurant establish a stronger and more recognizable digital identity.

---

## Design Challenge

How might we create a website that enables customers to easily discover, explore, and order from the restaurant while helping the business strengthen its independent online presence?

---

## Success Criteria

This project will be considered successful if the proposed design:

* Makes restaurant information easier to access.
* Improves menu discoverability.
* Creates a more cohesive customer experience.
* Establishes a stronger visual identity for the restaurant.
* Provides clear pathways to ordering and contact information.
* Supports both user needs and business goals.
