# Project Overview

This project is a UX/UI case study focused on designing a complete digital experience for an independent Chinese restaurant that currently relies primarily on third-party delivery platforms such as DoorDash and Grubhub for its online presence.

At the moment, customers can discover and order from the restaurant through these platforms, but there is no dedicated website that represents the restaurant’s brand, menu, and story in a centralized and controlled way. This creates a fragmented user experience where important information such as menu items, pricing context, restaurant background, and updates are distributed across multiple services rather than being owned by the restaurant itself.

The goal of this project is to design a responsive website that improves how customers discover, explore, and interact with the restaurant online, while also helping the business establish a stronger and more consistent digital identity.

From a user perspective, the experience is designed to reduce friction when trying to find key information such as menu items, hours, location, and ordering options. From a business perspective, the website aims to strengthen brand presence, improve discoverability, and create a more direct relationship with customers outside of third-party platforms.

The final outcome of this project includes a complete UX/UI design created in Figma, supported by research, user flows, wireframes, a design system, and high-fidelity interactive prototypes. The process is documented in this repository to demonstrate not only the final designs, but also the reasoning and iteration behind them.

This project is part of a UX/UI learning journey and is intended to demonstrate practical application of user-centered design principles in a real-world business context.
